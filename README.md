👋 About Me
I am Md. Ahsanur Rahaman, a Computer Science and Engineering student at Pundra University of Science & Technology, Bogura, with a strong interest in Artificial Intelligence, Data Science, Data Analytics, Machine Learning, Deep Learning, and Brain-Computer Interfaces (BCIs).
My research focuses on the intersection of AI, neural signal processing, data-driven methods, and intelligent healthcare, particularly:
- 🧠 EEG-based Motor Imagery
- 🔬 Neural Signal Analysis
- 🤖 Machine Learning & Deep Learning
- 🧬 Healthy-like EEG Reconstruction
- 🎨 Generative AI for Biomedical Data
- 🩺 AI-assisted Neurorehabilitation
- 📊 Data Analysis & Visualization
I enjoy turning complex datasets and ideas into practical, research-oriented computational solutions.
Current Goal: Pursue a research-focused Master's degree in AI, Data Science, Data Analytics, or Neural Engineering and contribute to intelligent healthcare research.
