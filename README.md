## 👋 About Me

Motivated **Computer Science & Engineering** student at **Pundra University of Science & Technology, Bogura** 🇧🇩 with a strong interest in **AI, Data Science, Data Analytics, and Brain-Computer Interfaces (BCIs)**.

My research sits at the intersection of **AI, neural signal processing, and intelligent healthcare**, with a focus on **EEG-based motor imagery** and **neurorehabilitation**. I enjoy turning complex biomedical data into meaningful insight through preprocessing, feature engineering, statistical analysis, machine learning, deep learning, and visualization.
